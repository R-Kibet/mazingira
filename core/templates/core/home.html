Sustainability Village

Mazingira Sustainability Village (MSV) is a registered community-led sustainability organization dedicated to advancing environmental conservation, climate action, green innovation, circular economy solutions, and community resilience. Our work is aligned with Kenya's Vision 2030, the National Climate Change Action Plan, the 15 Billion Trees Programme, and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
